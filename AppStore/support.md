# Logan Quest Support

Logan Quest turns a walk around downtown Logan, Utah, into a scavenger hunt. Walk to each stop,
solve its riddle, and open the prize box once you've found them all.

Need help? Email [erulemath@gmail.com](mailto:erulemath@gmail.com).

## Common questions

**My answer isn't working.**
Look around the stop again: the answer is something you can see from there. Stuck on the
riddle? Tap **Show Hint** to see it asked as a plain question. Capitals, punctuation, accents
and a leading "the" don't matter.

**How do I get to the next stop?**
Tap the stop, then **Walking Directions** to open it in Apple Maps. The stops are in walking
order, and the loop ends back where it started.

**Can I play Medium or Hard?**
Not yet. They're coming soon. Once they open, the stops you've found on Easy will count on them
too.

**How do I use the coupon?**
Open the prize box and show the coupon at the business's counter before the date on it. The
clock on the coupon ticks, which shows it's the app and not a screenshot.

**How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?**
Tap **Sign in with Apple** in the app on both iPhones, using the same Apple Account, with
iCloud turned on. Your stops and badges come along.

**I deleted the app. Is my progress gone?**
No. Download it again and your progress, badges and sign-in are still there.

**How do I start over?**
Go to My Progress → **Reset This Quest** or **Reset All Progress**. Your badges stay.

**What does the app do with my information?**
Nothing leaves your iPhone except to your own iCloud. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).
