# Logan Quest Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 22, 2026*

Logan Quest is a scavenger-hunt app for walking around Logan, Utah. We don't collect any
information about you. The app has no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it
never asks for your location. What it saves stays on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you
sign in.

## What the app saves

- **Your progress:** which stops you've found and when, the badges you've earned and when, and
  the difficulty you picked for each quest.
- **If you sign in with Apple:** the identifier Apple gives the app for your Apple Account, and
  your first name if you choose to share it, so the app can show it on the quest complete
  screen. The app never sees your email address.

## Where it's kept

- **On your iPhone.** Your progress and sign-in are saved in the app's storage and in your
  iPhone's keychain. The keychain copy stays on your iPhone if you delete the app, so your
  progress is still there if you download it again.
- **In your iCloud, if you sign in.** Your progress, badges and first name are also saved in
  your iCloud account, so they show up on any iPhone where you use Logan Quest. Only your own
  devices can read them. We can't.

We never receive any of this. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or
shared.

## Apple services

Sign in with Apple, iCloud and the map in the app are provided by Apple. The **Walking
Directions** button opens the Apple Maps app. Apple handles the data those services use under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## The prize coupon

Showing the coupon at a business's counter shares only what's on your screen. The app doesn't
tell the business, or us, who you are or that you redeemed it.

## Children

Logan Quest is for all ages. Because it doesn't collect information from anyone, it doesn't
collect any from children.

## Your choices

- **Sign out:** in the app, go to My Progress → Sign Out. Your identifier and name are removed
  from your iPhone, and your progress stays on your iPhone but stops saving to iCloud. Your
  first name stays in your iCloud so the app can greet you if you sign in again.
- **Stop using Sign in with Apple:** in the Settings app, open your Apple Account and find
  Logan Quest in the list of apps using Sign in with Apple. The app signs you out the next
  time it opens.
- **Erase your progress:** in the app, go to My Progress → Reset All Progress (or Reset This
  Quest). This erases your found stops on this iPhone and, if you're signed in, in iCloud and
  on your other devices. Badges are kept on purpose and can't be erased in the app.
- **Deleting the app** doesn't erase your progress, because of the keychain copy. Reset your
  progress first if you want it gone.
